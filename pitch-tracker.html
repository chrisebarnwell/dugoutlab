      Privacy
      • Adults only. A parent or coach age 18+ holds the account. Kids never get an account.
      • What we store. Your name and email. For each kid: a first name or nickname and age (coaches add a jersey number). For each session: date, Home, Practice, or Game, and the pitch, strike, and ball counts.
      • What we never collect. No kid email, last name, photo, school, or location.
      • Who sees it. Only you see your kids. Parents never see other families. Coaches see only their own team.
      • Deleting. Delete a player and every session from their stats page, or your whole account from your home screen. Done right away.
      • We use your email to save your data and send occasional DugoutLab updates. Never sold.
      Guidelines & sources
      • MLB and USA Baseball, Pitch Smart: Pitching Guidelines for Youth and Adolescent Pitchers. Daily max and rest days by age. Checked October 2026.
      • Pitch Smart numbers are written for game pitches. We count every pitch logged here that day, home and practice included, to stay on the safe side.
      • Pitch Smart's chart starts at ages 7 to 8. For 6-year-olds we show the 7 to 8 numbers.
      • This tool counts pitches. It is not medical advice. Warnings never block you. Any pain or soreness, stop and talk to a doctor.
