Insta Measure

Pick your league. Lock your phone on the back point of home plate. Aim at every base and the mound.

Step 01 · Pick your league
Which field are we building?
Bases60 ft
Mound46 ft
    Every distance runs from the back point of home plate. No tape? A big step is about 3 ft.
    Step 02 · Lock it and aim
    Lock the plate. Lock the line. Aim at every spot.
    LOCK 1+ on the back point of home plate
    LOCK 2+ on 2nd base, or straight out over the plate
    AIMTurn until the + goes gold. Tape it.
    1. Stand 3 to 6 ft behind home plate, phone at your chest, held upright.
    2. Put the + on the back point of the plate. Tap Lock plate.
    3. Put the + on 2nd base. No 2nd base yet? Aim straight out over the point of the plate. Tap Lock line.
    4. Pick a spot. Turn until the + goes gold. That is the exact line from the back point of the plate.
    5. Stay where you are. A helper walks that line with the tape. You keep them on the +.
    The phone measures turn and tilt well. It cannot tell when you walk. So you stay put and the tape goes out. Moved? Tap Re-lock. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves your phone.
    Phone height
    Chest height for most adults is 4 to 5 ft. It only sets how far the plate is from you, so close is good enough.
      Field is up
      Now play.

      Bases down, kids running. Small-sided games teach the skill for you.

      Guidelines & sources
      • Little League International, Field Specifications. Minors and Majors baseball 60 ft bases and 46 ft pitching. Intermediate 50/70 uses 70 ft bases and 50 ft pitching as its local option. Junior and up 90 ft and 60 ft 6 in. Softball 60 ft bases at every level, 35 ft Minors, 40 ft Majors, 43 ft Junior and Senior. Tee Ball 60 ft, with 50 ft as a local option.
      • NFHS high school rules. Baseball 90 ft and 60 ft 6 in. Fastpitch softball 60 ft and 43 ft.
      • Travel baseball (9U to 13U). 65/46, 70/50, and 80/54 are common travel distances. They change by organization, host, and year. Not checked against one rulebook. Flagged uncertain. Use the host's rules.
      • 8U softball. Little League Minors softball uses 35 ft. Many rec and travel leagues use 30 ft at 8U. Flagged uncertain. Pick Custom if your league differs.
      • Base placement. In the official rules diagram, first and third bags sit inside the lines in fair territory, and second base is centered on its point. Check your league book.
      • Phone sight. Plain geometry from your phone's tilt and turn sensors. Lock 1 finds the back point of the plate from your phone height and the down angle (close up, a steep angle, so small errors stay small). Lock 2 sets the line to 2nd. Every spot is then turned and tilted from that point by the league distances. Turn is good to about a degree over a few minutes and can drift, so tap Re-lock if the plate marker slides off. The phone cannot track you walking, so the tape decides distance. Pins away from the center of the screen use an assumed camera lens and are approximate. The gold + at the center is the exact line. Not field tested against a survey. DugoutLab build.
