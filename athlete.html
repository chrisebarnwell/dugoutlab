Athlete Plan

Train the athlete before the sport.

Eight quick questions build a plan from our guidelines: bodyweight first, land and stick, play on purpose. Log what actually happened, check in once a week, and the plan adjusts with a reason you can read.

How it works
Answer. Move. Log. Check in.

The plan comes from the Development Guide and nothing else. No throwing work, ever. Growth spurts and sore spots are things you report; the plan listens and holds. It never diagnoses and never clears anyone to play.
