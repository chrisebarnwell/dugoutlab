Tools for tonight.

Built for the night before practice. Ideas to try, not perfect answers.

The Hit Window
Turn cage work into a game. Targets go where real hits land. Then play: ten balls, call your shot, challenge anyone.
Pitch Tracker
Tap strike or ball. Count pitches, watch strike percent over time, and get a heads-up near the daily limit.
Strike Zone in AR
Size the zone to your hitter. Drop it over home plate with your phone and see it from the mound, the catcher's spot, or the side.
Lay Out the Field
Pick your league. Your phone drops a true-size diamond on the grass. Walk out and place every bag.
