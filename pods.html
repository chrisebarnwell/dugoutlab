Pod System

How pods work

Two halves of the field. Every kid lives on one side all game, then flips next game.

Right pod 1B · 2B · RF · CF

About 4 of every 7 kids. New spot every inning.

Left pod 3B · SS · LF

About 3 of every 7 kids. New spot every inning.

Pitcher and catcher a turn, not a pod

Step out of your pod to pitch or catch. Step back into the same pod when you are done. Not every kid pitches or catches every week.

Rotation
  • Every kid stays in Right or Left all game
  • New spot every inning inside your pod
  • Only P and C can repeat an inning
  • The inning before you pitch, you sit to get loose
Sitting
  • Each inning: P and C, 7 pod spots, the rest sit
  • Nobody sits two innings in a row
  • Warming up counts as your sit
  • Sits spread as evenly as the roster allows

Game 1: Right→Game 2: Left Every kid flips sides. Nobody owns a position.

Why pods. A kid needs a few innings on the same side of the field to stop thinking about where to stand and start reading the play. Right field backs up first. Left field backs up third. One side all game means the same angles and the same jobs. Flipping next game means they learn both. Nobody gets a permanent label. Our coaching call from the dugout, not a study.
