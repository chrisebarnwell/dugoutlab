My Age Group Tools Field Notes Why It Works
Lineup Builder · Pod Rotation

Two pods. Every kid, every spot.

Split the field in two. Kids rotate inside their pod, sit once at most in a row, and switch sides next game. Runs in your browser. Your roster is saved in this browser. If you email a lineup, we send that lineup and your email address for delivery.

Mode A · Development
Pod Rotation
Rec and younger teams. Everybody plays everywhere over the season. You set who pitches and catches.
Mode B · Competitive
Strike-Thrower Builder
All-Stars and travel. Tiers, a deeper pitching staff, and win / balanced / development priorities.

How pods work

+-------------------------------+ +--------------------------+ | RIGHT POD 1B 2B RF CF | | LEFT POD 3B SS LF | | about 4/7 of the roster | | about 3/7 of the roster | +---------------+---------------+ +------------+-------------+ | P / C step out for their turn | +-----------> return to same pod <----+ EACH INNING new spot (only P and C may repeat) pitcher-up-next sits to get loose nobody sits back-to-back NEXT GAME every kid switches sides
