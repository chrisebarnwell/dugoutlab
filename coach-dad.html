Coach Dad

The hardest player you coach lives in your house. This is a short daily note for the man in the third-base box who is also the man at the dinner table.

You learned to hold your tongue with another man's kid a long time ago. You praise effort. You stay calm after the error. You make the quiet one feel seen. Then you get home, and the kid who shares your last name gets the worn-out version of you, the one with no patience left.

Coach Dad is about closing that gap. One short reflection a day, six weeks, on the single most powerful tool you own and the one you guard the least: your words. It draws on old wisdom about the weight of the tongue and modern work on intentional living, and it points all of it at the field and the family.

What you get
  • One short email each morning. Two minutes to read.
  • A single idea, a single line of old wisdom, and one thing to try that day with your team and your kids.
  • Six weeks. Then you decide if you want what comes next.
  • No cost. No pitch. Unsubscribe in one click, anytime.
