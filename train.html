Train

Train on your own.

Two ways to get better between practices. Solo physical drills for the field or the backyard, and brain-training games that sharpen pitch recognition, decisions, and reaction speed.

Batter Up

A pitch comes in. If it's in the dashed strike zone, swing. If it's outside, take it. Swing too early or too late and you'll miss. Read it. React.

What this trains

Pitch recognition is the single biggest separator between hitters. Good hitters aren't swinging faster, they're deciding faster whether a pitch is worth swinging at.

Reaction speed is trainable. Quick hands start with quick eyes. This sharpens the link between seeing and acting.
