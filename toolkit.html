Tool 01 · End-of-Season

The Awards Deck

POWERPOINT (.PPTX) · 8.5 × 11 PORTRAIT · 13 SLIDES

A fully editable awards deck for your end-of-season banquet. 11 award categories covering effort, heart, growth, baseball IQ, and signature moments. Each slide gives you the award title, a kicker line, suggested variations, and four ready-to-use blurb ideas. Print on letter-size paper, frame them, hand them out.

What's Inside
  • The Hustle Award · The Bear Award
  • Heart of the Team · The Encourager
  • Most Improved · The Sponge
  • The Heads-Up Award · The Quiet Pro
  • The Walk-Off · Best Smile in the Dugout
  • Coach's Award

Tool 02 · Pre-Game

The Scouting Report

FILLABLE PDF · SINGLE PAGE · 69 FIELDS

A one-page scouting form your assistant coach can fill in during warmups. Type into it on your phone or print it for the clipboard. Game info, pitcher tendencies, all 9 hitters, defense and baserunning, plus a "what we attack / what we respect" section at the bottom. Built for rec coaches, not pro scouts.

What's Inside
  • Game info: team, opponent, date, location
  • Pitcher: hand, velo, arsenal, behind-in-count tendency
  • Opposing hitters 1-9: name, hand, type, pitch them in, avoid, notes
  • Their team: defense and baserunning notes
  • Game plan: what we attack, what we respect
